### The risk engine
- **Readings belong in the risk engine, not beside it.** User, 2026-11-16: "all these factors need to build towards that risk score"; 2026-11-18: "are we building these as pieces of the modules for risk engine?" Today the buy and sell chains hold separate steps next to the risk score (`perp_worth`, `calm`, the shadow `shape`, the risk trend), each with its own yes or no. The shape the user described is: the lane's gates say what is eligible, the risk engine gives one number for the side from all its readings, and that number is what the buy and the sale both ask. So a reading that proves itself in shadow (the look-back shape, the Kalshi perp's agreement, the calm state) becomes a reading of the risk score (`riskscore.NAMES`, fitted with the rest), and its separate step is removed.
- **The risk score does not see flip risk on a late favourite.** On 26,543 held-out moments of a side at 0.80 to 0.92, the score's view against Kalshi's price did not separate flips (2.1% / 2.3% / 1.8% by thirds); the time left did (0.6% over 7 min, 2.7% at 3 to 7, 10.5% inside 3 min). `studies/results/2026-11-18-risk-score-flip-check.txt`. Make flip risk a reading the score carries (the share of the remaining time a fall of that size needs), then rerun this check.
- **A static volatility number in the exit.** The exit's "typical minute" of the price (`quoteshock.typical_minute`) is one number: the last 15 minutes' one-second moves, read every 5 s, then scaled by sqrt(time left) in the profit take's bar, the sure-hold release, the nudge and the trail noise (`curverisk.py`, `watcher.py`). On the tape the price's one-second moves GROW as the close nears: 1.9c at 7 to 10 min left, 4.8c at 1 to 2 min, 10.2c in the last 30 s (`studies/results/2026-11-18-volatility-by-time-left.txt`). So late in a market the number is a calm trailing average and the sqrt scaling shrinks it further: tolerances are too tight exactly when moves are largest. Possible cause of the trail selling winners (16 of 21 would have won since 11-17). Replace with a time-aware price volatility (the price's own move implied by its cushion and the time left), and check the trail again. The spot-based cushion (`curverisk.py` line 167) scales spot moves by sqrt(time), which is right for spot: only the price-based unit is affected. Not checked: `max_vol_rel`.
  - 2026-11-18, step 1 done (shadow): `pricevol.py` has four estimates of the price's next-30-s one-second move; held out on 7 days (151,485 marks, `studies/results/2026-11-18-price-vol-estimators.txt`) the model-implied one (`implied_rate`: phi(z) x the cushion's own rate / sqrt(left)) is closest: mean absolute log ratio 0.530 against 0.858 for the static unit (0.460 against 0.812 with one scale fitted on earlier days), and it is right in the rms sense overall (realized/estimated 0.98; static 1.14, and 1.36 at 1.5 to 3 min left, 1.37 near even). At 1.5 to 3 min the mixed one is a little closer (0.697 against 0.749). Sell step `trail_timed` (shadow, never sells) records the trail's verdict with both units (`scripts/trail_timed_score.py` scores it forward). Not yet in any sale; the profit take's bar, the sure-hold release and the squeeze reach still read the static unit.
