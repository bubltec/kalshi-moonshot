PriskP checks model: its buys on the recorded tape (crypto)

The model, as run: five yes/no checks (cushion, 1-3 min trend, 30-min history, Kalshi price trend, price dip), each read in its own-history fifth (the series' previous 24 h, same 3 minutes of the market). NO = fifths 1-2, WEAK = 3, YES = 4-5. No buy at a reading where any check says NO, WEAK or anomaly. The buy is a take at the side's ask at the first 10-second reading inside the window where all five say YES. "Plain" = the favourite side at the first usable reading in the window, no checks; "refused" = markets where the plain buy exists and the model never buys. Every buy held to settlement, $10 a buy, fee on both legs. Dollars are driven partly by price (cheap buys win more dollars): read "ahead" against "need" (price plus fee).
