Fill model calibration (scripts/fill_model_calibration.py; 1,170 live attempts since 2026-11-02 with quotes on the tape: 1,102 filled, 68 not). An order is stamped within milliseconds of its decision (median 0.0 s), so the model takes the best ask of the order's own side from the first quote at or after the decision and fills it when it is within the lane's limit (decision price plus its allowance).
Ordinary lanes (latency 0): commodity_industrial 92% correct, 0.4-0.8c price error; commodity_precious 92%, 0.43c; crypto_favorite 96%, 0.62c; crypto_btc 100%, 0.14c; crypto_sol 96%, 0.41c; crypto_wild_fade 100%, 0.44c; treasury_favorite 85%, 0.70c; fx_favorite 91%, 4.6c (thin books). Waiting 1-4 s before the quote made every lane worse (1.2-2.9c of error).
Burst lanes: crypto_moonshot 70% correct (model 61% fills against 52% actual), energy_moonshot 64% (67% against 42%); a depth rule (the book must show 0.5-1.5x the stake at the ask) and a quote window of 0-3 after the order did not get either above 69%. The cheap side is gone before the quote tape shows it, so no paper twin of a burst lane can be trusted.
What was built: fillmodel.simulate_taker_buy; paperfill.settle_taker (a lane's paper bets are refilled at the tape's ask or turned into a pass, set per lane with paper_fill_model); misses.py and `python -m kalshi_moonshot misses` (live fills against the orders that did not fill, with the outcome of both). On 11-04: energy_moonshot filled 14 (1 won, -$1.11) and missed 26 (12 won, +$16.02 if filled); crypto_moonshot filled 129 (24 won, +$139.65) and missed 100 (8 won, -$17.14).
