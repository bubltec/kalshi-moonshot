Swing-turn test (scripts/swing_turn_test.py): every confirmed turn of the spot (a retrace of 1.5 typical minutes off an extreme) since 11-03, 15,832 turns, traded on real quotes: buy the side that moves with the turn at the ask, sell at the bid at +0.06/+0.10/+0.15 or at the time limit (90-180 s); cents per contract after the fee both ways.
Persistence: more swings in the last 15 minutes mean modestly more in the next 5 (crypto 1.57 pivots after <=3 swings, 1.90 after 8+; commodities 1.39 vs 1.82).
Trading the confirmed turn: every setting loses, -2.3c to -6.4c per contract, in both halves, with the turn or against it, at every regularity level (<=3, 4-5, 6-7, 8+ swings in 15 minutes). Hit rates 43-63%, but a +6c to +15c target barely covers the ~4.3c cost.
The ceiling: buying at the exact swing low/high in hindsight and selling at the next extreme makes +32c (crypto) and +29c (commodities) per contract, 98-100% profitable. The whole gain is in knowing the turn before it is confirmed.
Fixed-hold scalps (scripts/hold_time_cost_test.py, 3,408 markets): buy at the ask, sell at the bid after 5-120 s: -3.9c to -4.6c per contract at every hold, momentum before the buy changes nothing; a round trip costs about 4.3c (spread + fee both ways).
