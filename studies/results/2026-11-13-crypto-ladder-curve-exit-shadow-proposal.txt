PROPOSAL, not applied: a paper twin of crypto_multiscale that buys on the ladder chance (priced from the Kalshi perp) and sells with the new curve risk exit.
(user, 2026-11-13: "i wanted to use ladder on it with the new curve exit". Nothing here is in data/strategies.toml; cloud_push.sh was not run; the live switch stays the user's.)

What crypto_multiscale does today (read from lanes.py, runner.py, estimators.py and data/strategies.toml):
  always = true  -> the ladder estimator returns the ask as the "chance" for crypto (kp: symbols), so edge is 0 and min_edge = 0.015 is never applied. estimator = "ladder" does nothing for the buy.
  the buy is decided by: price 0.70-0.90, spread <= 0.04, minutes 5-15; buy_flat_trend_pct = 0.05 (sit out while the market-wide 60-minute trend is within 0.05%); buy_curve_edge_min = -0.05 (the spot curve's chance no more than 0.05 under the ask); model_buy with model_top = 0.30, model_features = "curvature" (the ridge's predicted net in the top 30% of training moments and above 0).

The twin (one buy change plus the exit, everything else copied from crypto_multiscale on the day it is added, as the other twins were):
  [lane.crypto_ladder_perp]
  enabled = true                 # paper: NOT in LIVE_LANES; the host's PAPER_LANE_NAMES must name it or PAPER_LANES=false switches it off
  series  = KXBTC15M, KXETH15M, KXSOL15M, KXXRP15M, KXDOGE15M   # the only coins with a kp: instrument (instruments.toml); ADA, BCH, BNB, HYPE, NEAR, ZEC have none: the ladder cannot price them and a non-always lane drops them ("could not price this ladder")
  always = false                 # the ladder chance is read: edge = chance - ask - fee, from kalshifeed (KP_*) through the window-offset pricer
  min_edge = 0.015               # the lane's own edge bar (lanes.wants_bet); see the evidence below for why this will buy rarely
  model_buy = false, buy_curve_edge_min = -9.0, buy_flat_trend_pct = -1.0   # the three curve gates off: the ladder is the buy
  curve_risk_sell = 0.5, curve_risk_live = true (a paper sale), curve_risk_spot_weight = 0.0, curve_risk_history = true, curve_risk_max_loss = 0.5, curve_risk_min_profit = 0.01   # the new exit as in crypto_multiscale's shadow today; the older curve_exit / time-to-go / shock sale fields set off on the twin so one handler sells (user: "the single handler for all exits")

What the evidence says about it (studies/results/2026-11-13-ladder-perp-buy.txt, in scope, 65 bets that could be priced, since 11-11):
  - at min_edge 0.015 the ladder keeps 2 of 65 bets (-4.28 booked, 0 won); the twin would almost never buy.
  - edge buckets: under -0.05: 45 bets won 81-85% at asks 0.80-0.82; -0.05 to 0.015: 18 bets won 66.7%; the ask beats the ladder chance (Brier 0.1506 against 0.1738): the pricer's width (6 hours of 5-minute bars x COMMODITY_VOL_MULT 1.25) is wide for a 15-minute market.
  - keeping every bet at edge -0.05 or better would keep about 20 of 65: about -14 booked, -7 held.
  So the ladder buy is not supported by the history; this twin is to collect forward evidence, scored against crypto_multiscale's bets (rule 5/6), not a recommendation to go live. Two stated choices to make before it is added: the edge bar (0.015 buys almost nothing; -0.05 matches the live lane's curve-edge allowance) and whether the width multiplier (a global setting) is touched at all; neither is chosen from these results.

UPDATE, 2026-11-13 later (user: "i thought we are trying to test the ladder"; "whatever you need to change to make the ladder work"; "Fit from recent outcomes"):
  - The edge bar stays the ladder's own: min_edge = 0.015 (not -0.05; the live lane's curve allowance is a different number on a different chance).
  - The ladder's width was the broken part, not the idea. On every settled crypto 15-minute market since 11-11 (studies/results/2026-11-13-ladder-calibration-all-markets.txt, 455 markets, 1741 readings) the width as set (global k 1.25) scored Brier 0.1322 against the quote's 0.1215; with a k fitted per series from its last 6 hours of outcomes, 0.1271. At the edge bar the kept bets returned +9.0% (39 bets, width as set) and +3.2% (153 bets, fitted width) against -3.7% for the whole 0.70-0.90 band (364 markets); the skipped ones -5.2% / -8.7%. Two days, one regime; held to settlement, entry fee only; the curve risk exit is not in that number.
  - Built (src/kalshi_moonshot/widthfit.py, commodities.CommodityPricer.learn_widths, price_event(widths=...), Settings.commodity_width_fit / env COMMODITY_WIDTH_FIT, default on): every series priced from the Kalshi perp feed (kp: instruments) refits its own multiplier hourly from the windows it settled in the last 6 hours, each read at minutes 5, 8, 11, 14 as the ladder would have priced it; under 40 readings, or a fit older than 3 hours, the global 1.25 stays. Commodity series (other feeds) are not touched. Of the lanes, only a non-always crypto lane (this twin) reads the ladder chance for crypto, so live crypto_multiscale (always = true, the ask) does not change.
