Crypto volatility regime and what to do in it (scripts/crypto_vol_regime_study.py, crypto_wild_fade_study.py, crypto_scalp_regime_study.py, crypto_fair_value_scalp_study.py; host tapes 2026-10-27 to 11-04, 2,781 crypto favorites; trailing 6 h realized volatility of the coin's settlement index relative to that coin's median over the period).

The weekend (10-31, 11-01) ran at 0.56x and 0.68x normal volatility; every day since at 1.1x to 1.8x. Market-wide crypto favorites held to settlement won 86% and 84% on Saturday and Sunday (+$149, +$46) and 80-83% on the weekdays (-$151, -$118, -$88). Live: Saturday +$63, Sunday +$138 (about half of it moonshot lottery wins), Monday to Wednesday -$2, -$96, -$13.
By trailing volatility: calm third (0.6x) 85% won, +$139; middle -$53; wild third (1.8x) 81% won, -$229. Below 0.7x: 667 favorites, 87% won, +$263 (+$0.39 a bet). Earlier conclusion that no regime exists was wrong: the 2.3 days then examined were all high volatility.

Scalps lose in every regime: taker -4.4c a trade and maker -4.6c in wild periods, -4.9c and -5.3c in calm (1c spread, but about 3.5c of fees plus the spread a round trip). A fair-value gap (the contract price against normal-distribution chance from the spot, the strike, the time left and the volatility) has no pull on the next 30 s (corr 0.013); scalping on it lost 3-4c a trade. Dip scalps inside each spot variety (A-G) lost 1.2c to 5.1c a trade; the best, B far and flat, was -1.2c on 61 trades. Scalping has no edge at these costs.

What works in wild periods: volatility at 1.3x and up and the favorite priced 0.82 or more, buy the underdog and hold to settlement. 356 setups: the underdog cost 13.1c and won 16.6% (95% interval 12.7-20.4%); net +$795 on $10 tickets, +$2.23 a bet, fees in. Positive in both halves (+$1.61 and +$2.86 a bet) and on 4 of 6 days (10-28 n=9 -$40, 11-02 n=40 -$132), and on all 5 coins. Flipping every other favorite: -$1.73 a bet. Caveats: thresholds picked after looking at the tables, a full-sample median, about 2 standard errors, one win in six.
