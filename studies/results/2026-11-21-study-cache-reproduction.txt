Why the numbers differ: the data is the same; the fit is not deterministic. Markets tick on the same minute grid, so many rows share a time, and the fit uses every third row (and the bar every fourth) of the rows sorted by time; with ties the order comes from multiprocessing imap_unordered, so each runner run subsamples different rows. Shuffling only the tie order on the cache moves half 2 from -0.040 to +0.066 and half 1's bar from +0.12 to +0.18. The runner's -0.039/+0.016 and the quoted -0.012/+0.053 both sit inside that spread. Any difference between two variants smaller than this (about 0.05-0.1 per half at n 75-200; the standard error alone is 0.04-0.08) is noise from the subsample, not a result. Use TIE=<seed> to see it; a fair comparison fits on every row or averages seeds. (Not changed in this commit: the fit scripts keep their subsample.)
